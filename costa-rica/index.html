Late October to Early November 2026

Three ways to balance adventure, water, and a genuinely relaxing Costa Rica escape.

Here are three Costa Rica trip directions for your late October to early November window. Each option is designed around no rental car, boutique hotels, water time, and a balance of active days and real downtime.

How to choose

A few notes behind the numbers.

The lowest subtotal is not automatically the best fit. These are the tradeoffs I would keep in mind as you compare the route options above.

Best perk value

El Mangroove includes breakfast, upgrade when available, early check-in / late check-out when available, and a 4th-night-style promo.

Best luxury anchor

Nayara prices well for a 4-night stay, making it a compelling Arenal anchor for the smoother route.

Best budget release valve

Lost Iguana is the easiest way to lower the subtotal while still staying in the Arenal area with breakfast included.

Beach tradeoff

Capitan Suizo gives you more Tamarindo access, while El Mangroove keeps the departure transfer shorter and includes the stronger set of benefits.

Adventure inclusion

Exploradores' 2-day Pacuare trip includes rafting, hikes, one night at Pacuare River Lodge, 2 breakfasts, 2 lunches, 1 dinner, insurance, and transportation from Puerto Viejo to La Fortuna town.

Weather Strategy

Why the order of the trip matters.

Costa Rica does not have one single weather pattern. For these dates, the Caribbean side is most interesting at the beginning of the trip, while the northern Pacific becomes more attractive as November progresses.

Puerto Viejo / Caribbean Best early in the trip

The Caribbean coast has a different rainfall rhythm from the Pacific, which is why Puerto Viejo works best at the beginning.

Pacuare / Turrialba Adventure-friendly, with backup needed

Rainforest and river conditions can make rafting exciting. Before booking, we would confirm the go/no-go timing and backup transportation plan.

La Fortuna / Arenal Rain-compatible experiences

Showers are common, but waterfalls, rainforest walks, and hot springs still work well. Plan key activities earlier in the day when possible.

Rio Celeste / Bijagua Lush, not weather-safe

This stop is beautiful and nature-forward, but it should be chosen for the blue river and rainforest setting rather than guaranteed sun.

Guanacaste / Papagayo / Tamarindo Improves as November unfolds

The Pacific side is coming out of rainy season. Ending here is the right strategy because Nov 4-8 is more promising than late October.

What this means Not rain-proof, but thoughtful

Option A is the most weather-strategic. Option C is the easiest and still sensible because it saves Guanacaste for the end.

Travel Flow

What the transportation days look like.

Since Seattle flights are likely to connect, the first day should stay simple: arrive into San Jose, sleep near the airport, and start the actual route the next morning.

Option A Caribbean + Adventure
  1. Arrive SJOSleep near the airport after the long travel day.
  2. SJO to Puerto ViejoAbout 4.5 to 5.5 hours. This is the longest regular drive of this route.
  3. Puerto Viejo to PacuareThe rafting outfitter picks up from Puerto Viejo for the overnight river experience.
  4. Pacuare to La FortunaThe rafting trip helps bridge the country, with onward transportation toward La Fortuna town included.
  5. La Fortuna to departure airportFinal airport should be chosen based on best flights; SJO and LIR are both possible from Arenal.
Option B Costa Rica Highlights
  1. Arrive SJOSleep near the airport, then begin the route fresh the next morning.
  2. SJO to La FortunaAbout 3 hours. This is the first real transfer of the trip.
  3. La Fortuna to Rio CelesteAbout 1.5 to 2 hours, keeping this middle move relatively manageable.
  4. Rio Celeste to beach hotelContinue west for the beach finish: roughly 1.5 to 2 hours to El Mangroove, or about 2.5 to 3 hours to Capitan Suizo.
  5. Guanacaste to LIROften 20 to 90 minutes, which makes departure day easier than returning to San Jose.
Option C Adventure + Beach
  1. Arrive SJOKeep the arrival night simple with an airport-area hotel.
  2. SJO to La FortunaAbout 3 hours. Settle into the Arenal portion first.
  3. La Fortuna to Guanacaste About 3 to 4 hours as a direct transfer, or turn this into an Arenal Evergreen Rio Celeste transfer-tour with an early pickup, park visit, guide, lunch, and drop-off in Guanacaste.
  4. Guanacaste beach timeNo additional major moves once you reach the beach portion.
  5. Guanacaste to LIRThe simplest departure flow, especially if choosing El Mangroove or another Papagayo-area stay.
