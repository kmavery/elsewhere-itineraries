Good to know

This is the most logistically optimized route, with one main in-trip transfer and an easy Liberia departure.

Rafting approach

Keep rafting as a one-day add-on from the Arenal, Tenorio, or Guanacaste area so it can stay flexible if the weather feels too wet.

Built-in experiences

Nayara adds rainforest/wildlife programming, Tabacon anchors the hot springs, El Mangroove is the strongest hotel-benefit play, and Capitan Suizo gives the most casual beach activity access.

Travel Flow

What the transportation days look like.

Since Seattle flights are likely to connect, the first day should stay simple: arrive into San Jose, sleep near the airport, and start the actual route the next morning.

Recommended route Arenal + Pacific Finale
  1. Arrive SJOKeep the arrival night simple with an airport-area hotel.
  2. SJO to La FortunaAbout 3 hours. Settle into the Arenal portion first.
  3. La Fortuna to Guanacaste About 3 to 4 hours as a direct transfer, or turn this into a private Arenal Evergreen Rio Celeste transfer-tour. Current quote for one passenger is $345 total to El Mangroove or $395 total to Capitan Suizo, taxes included.
  4. Transfer termsPrivate door-to-door service; deposit required; cancellations at least 24 hours before service are refundable less a 5% processing or bank fee.
  5. Guanacaste beach timeNo additional major moves once you reach the beach portion. Rafting can be priced as a one-day add-on rather than an overnight commitment.
  6. Guanacaste to LIRThe simplest departure flow, especially if choosing El Mangroove or another Papagayo-area stay.
