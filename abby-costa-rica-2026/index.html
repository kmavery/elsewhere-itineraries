Arenal Hanging Bridges

A 2-mile guided circuit through a 600-acre reserve with 10 fixed and 6 hanging bridges, reaching up to 180 feet. The step-free, non-slip trail has a gentle maximum grade. Includes transport, admission, naturalist guide, fruit, and water.

Jacamar · Half day+ · $92.66 incl. VAT

Class II–III rafting

The day’s river section is chosen for the best conditions, with at least 30 Class II–III rapids. Includes transport, equipment, raft guide, safety kayaker, fruit, water, and lunch at La Finquita. Secure closed shoes and a change of clothes are recommended.

Jacamar · Half day · $91.53 incl. VAT

La Fortuna Waterfall & La Finquita Farm

The waterfall rises 230 feet; choose the viewpoint or descend 530 steps to the base, with swimming possible when conditions allow. Jacamar’s quoted outing pairs the waterfall with La Finquita Farm and includes transport and guiding.

Jacamar · Half day · $79.10 incl. VAT

Arenal Volcano Hike

Follow steeper, sandy trails through regenerating forest and over petrified lava, with views toward Lake Arenal. Includes transport, admission, naturalist guide, fruit, and water. Best suited to travelers comfortable on uneven terrain.

Jacamar · Half day · $91.53 incl. VAT

Safari float on the Peñas Blancas River

A slow, stable raft trip with minimal paddling and strong wildlife-viewing potential, including monkeys, sloths, iguanas, turtles, caimans, and birds. Includes transport, naturalist guide, flotation device, boat, snacks, water, and admission.

Jacamar · Half day · $75.71 incl. VAT

La Finquita Rainforest Night Walk

A guided evening search for nocturnal wildlife such as bats, frogs, sloths, insects, and—occasionally—snakes. Includes transport, admission, lanterns, a snack, and naturalist guide. Closed shoes or boots are recommended.
